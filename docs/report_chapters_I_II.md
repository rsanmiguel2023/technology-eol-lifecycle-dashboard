# Report Chapters I–II

## Chapter I: Introduction
Technology lifecycle management is a key control area for banking operations, cybersecurity, compliance, and financial planning. Unsupported hardware and software can expose the organization to security vulnerabilities, audit findings, business disruption, and unplanned capital spend.

## Chapter II: Data and Methodology
The analysis uses synthetic enterprise inventory and operational records modeled after a large banking environment. The methodology includes ETL, cleaning, feature engineering, descriptive analytics, comparative analysis, risk scoring, and executive dashboard reporting.
