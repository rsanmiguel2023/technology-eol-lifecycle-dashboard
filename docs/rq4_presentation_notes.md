# RQ4: Refresh Budget Planning — Presentation Notes

Open with the research question: How much budget is needed for refresh planning?

Plain-English message: EOL risk becomes actionable when translated into funding needs. This page converts lifecycle exposure into refresh cost by year, asset type, and region.

Recommended management action: Approve a risk-based refresh budget. Fund Past EOL and 0–12 month assets first, then schedule supported assets into future refresh waves.
