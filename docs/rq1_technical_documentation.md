# RQ1: Assets Already Past EOL

## Research Question
Which assets are already past End of Life?

## Hypotheses
**H0:** There is no significant concentration of unsupported assets within the technology estate.

**H1:** A significant concentration of unsupported assets exists and creates elevated operational, cybersecurity, and compliance risk.

## Executive Summary
The first executive priority is to identify assets that have already passed vendor support. These assets should be treated as active risk items, not ordinary inventory records.

## Methodology
Classify assets by Lifecycle_Status, count Past EOL assets, calculate replacement cost, identify affected asset types and regions, and rank assets by Risk_Score.

## Interpretation Guide
A Past EOL asset means the bank may no longer receive normal vendor fixes, firmware updates, compatibility support, or security updates. The management question is not only how many assets are unsupported, but whether those assets support critical business services.

## Recommended Actions
Create a Past EOL remediation queue. Prioritize critical assets, assets with vulnerabilities, and assets with incident history before lower-risk refresh candidates.

## Tooltip Definition
Past EOL means the asset is beyond expected lifecycle support. For executives, this is a risk and funding prioritization view, not only an inventory count.
