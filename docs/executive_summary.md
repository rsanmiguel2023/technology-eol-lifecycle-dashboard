# Executive Summary

## Technology Lifecycle Governance Dashboard

Maple Financial Bank maintains **23,770 managed technology assets** across end-user computing, infrastructure, network, cloud, and enterprise application environments. The current lifecycle assessment identifies **4,682 assets already past vendor support**, representing **19.7%** of the managed estate.

The near-term refresh pipeline is material: **8,337 assets expire within 12 months**, **11,704 assets require action within 24 months**, and **17,106 assets require action within 36 months**. This creates a multi-year funding, sequencing, and governance requirement rather than a one-time hardware replacement exercise.

Cybersecurity exposure is concentrated in **1,391 unsupported or near-EOL assets with critical or high vulnerability exposure**. Laptop assets represent the largest concentration, but the exposure also includes servers, network devices, desktops, VDI, and storage assets. This makes the risk cross-platform, not endpoint-only.

Software lifecycle analysis identifies **91,618 unsupported software installations**. The largest compliance drivers include **Adobe Acrobat Pro 2020, Google Chrome, Microsoft Teams Classic, Windows 10 Enterprise, OneDrive**. These technologies should be prioritized because unsupported software can create audit, vendor-support, and cyber hygiene exposure.

Refresh planning estimates **CAD 70.8M in required investment across FY2026–FY2029**. The largest forecast year is **FY2027**, requiring approximately **CAD 25.6M**.

The highest-risk business unit is **Retail Banking**, with an executive risk index of **110.0** and a **Critical** risk band. This business unit has the highest combined exposure across unsupported assets, vulnerability concentration, downtime, and replacement cost.

## Executive Decision Points

- Prioritize unsupported assets with critical and high vulnerabilities.
- Approve a phased refresh roadmap instead of funding replacements reactively.
- Use business-unit risk ranking to sequence remediation.
- Reduce unsupported software in parallel with hardware refresh.
- Establish recurring lifecycle governance, exception tracking, and executive reporting.
