# RQ6: Incidents and Downtime

## Research Question
How does EOL status relate to incidents and downtime?

## Hypotheses
**H0:** EOL status has no relationship with incident frequency or downtime.

**H1:** EOL assets experience higher incident frequency and/or downtime and create operational resilience risk.

## Executive Summary
Lifecycle risk should be connected to operational outcomes. This page compares incident rates and downtime across lifecycle status groups.

## Methodology
Aggregate incident count and downtime by Lifecycle_Status, then calculate incident rate per asset and downtime per asset.

## Interpretation Guide
If Past EOL or near-EOL assets show higher downtime, lifecycle refresh can be justified not only as compliance work but as an operational resilience investment.

## Recommended Actions
Use downtime evidence to support refresh sequencing. Prioritize assets that are unsupported, business-critical, and linked to repeated incidents.

## Tooltip Definition
Incident and downtime analysis connects lifecycle management to service reliability and business disruption.
