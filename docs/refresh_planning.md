# Refresh Planning

## Business Question

How much investment is required to reduce lifecycle risk?

## Key Findings

- Estimated refresh investment across FY2026–FY2029: **CAD 70.8M**.
- Peak funding year: **FY2027**, with approximately **CAD 25.6M** required.
- FY2026 requires immediate planning because it includes the current past-EOL population.

## Interpretation

The refresh requirement is a multi-year investment roadmap. Deferring refresh activity increases the chance of higher future remediation costs, emergency replacements, security exceptions, and operational disruption.

## Business Impact

The recommended approach is phased funding. The bank should fund the immediate past-EOL population first, then sequence 12-, 24-, and 36-month refresh windows through normal fiscal planning cycles.
