# Lifecycle Exposure

## Business Question

Where is unsupported and near-end-of-life technology concentrated across the organization?

## Key Findings

- **4,682 assets** are already past vendor support.
- **8,337 assets** expire within 12 months.
- **11,704 assets** require refresh planning within 24 months.
- **17,106 assets** require refresh planning within 36 months.
- **Retail Banking** is the highest-risk business unit, followed by **Technology**.

## Interpretation

Lifecycle risk is not evenly distributed. Retail Banking has the largest managed asset footprint and the highest combined exposure across unsupported assets, vulnerability concentration, downtime, and replacement cost. Technology is also a high-priority risk area because it supports enterprise platforms and operational delivery.

## Business Impact

The near-term refresh pipeline should be sequenced by business criticality and risk concentration. Treating every asset equally would dilute resources and delay remediation in the areas where operational and cyber exposure are highest.
