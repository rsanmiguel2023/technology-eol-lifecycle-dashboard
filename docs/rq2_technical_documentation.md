# RQ2: Business Unit EOL Exposure

## Research Question
Which business units have the highest EOL exposure?

## Hypotheses
**H0:** EOL exposure is evenly distributed across business units.

**H1:** Certain business units carry disproportionately high EOL exposure and require targeted refresh planning.

## Executive Summary
Business unit ownership matters because refresh planning requires accountability. This page shows where risk sits organizationally and where leadership action is required.

## Methodology
Aggregate assets by Business_Unit, compute Past EOL counts, Past EOL rate, replacement cost, critical vulnerabilities, and downtime.

## Interpretation Guide
A business unit with high EOL rate and high replacement cost may need a dedicated refresh wave. A unit with lower volume but high critical vulnerability exposure may need immediate risk remediation.

## Recommended Actions
Assign remediation owners by business unit. Review top exposed units monthly until Past EOL exposure is reduced to an acceptable threshold.

## Tooltip Definition
EOL exposure by business unit helps leadership assign accountability, prioritize funding, and avoid treating lifecycle risk as only an IT inventory issue.
