# RQ6: Incidents and Downtime — Presentation Notes

Open with the research question: How does EOL status relate to incidents and downtime?

Plain-English message: Lifecycle risk should be connected to operational outcomes. This page compares incident rates and downtime across lifecycle status groups.

Recommended management action: Use downtime evidence to support refresh sequencing. Prioritize assets that are unsupported, business-critical, and linked to repeated incidents.
