# RQ4: Refresh Budget Planning

## Research Question
How much budget is needed for refresh planning?

## Hypotheses
**H0:** Current refresh funding is sufficient to address EOL exposure.

**H1:** Additional or reallocated investment is required to reduce EOL exposure within acceptable timelines.

## Executive Summary
EOL risk becomes actionable when translated into funding needs. This page converts lifecycle exposure into refresh cost by year, asset type, and region.

## Methodology
Use Expected_EOL, Asset_Type, Region, and Replacement_Cost_CAD to forecast refresh demand by year and compare expected needs against budget planning tables.

## Interpretation Guide
A budget spike in one refresh year indicates deferred lifecycle risk. Finance and Technology should smooth the refresh schedule where possible while still prioritizing high-risk unsupported assets.

## Recommended Actions
Approve a risk-based refresh budget. Fund Past EOL and 0–12 month assets first, then schedule supported assets into future refresh waves.

## Tooltip Definition
Refresh budget converts risk into investment required. It helps executives decide how much funding is needed and when.
